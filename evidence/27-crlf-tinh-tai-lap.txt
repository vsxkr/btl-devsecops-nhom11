NGUYEN NHAN
  Thong diep loi noi khong thay SCRIPT, nhung thuc te la khong thay INTERPRETER.
  Tep co ket thuc dong CRLF nen shebang duoc doc thanh "#!/bin/bash\r", va kernel
  di tim "/bin/bash\r".

  $ head -1 app/InlaneWeb/db/fix-perms-entrypoint.sh | cat -A
  #!/bin/bash^M$

  $ git -C ../PTIT-DevOps-SonarQube config --get core.autocrlf
  true

  Repo goc dat core.autocrlf=true, nen moi tep text duoc checkout ra voi CRLF tren
  Windows. Dockerfile COPY tep do vao anh, va anh mang theo CRLF.
